# JenesisAI — The Cognitive J

## Brand idea

The JenesisAI symbol is called **The Cognitive J**. It brings three ideas into one continuous mark:

- **The brain** represents intelligence, memory, reasoning, and discovery.
- **The center seam** represents thought becoming structured and directed.
- **The J** represents JenesisAI and carries intelligence into purposeful action.

The visual journey moves from cognition into creation. It expresses the central promise of JenesisAI: **intelligence transformed into useful work.**

The mark also reflects the name “Jenesis”—the beginning of an idea and the moment it becomes something real.

## Short brand statement

**The Cognitive J turns intelligence into action in one unmistakable symbol.**

## Expanded story

JenesisAI is designed to do more than produce answers. It helps people think, explore, organize, create, and move ideas forward.

The two brain hemispheres communicate advanced intelligence without relying on a generic sparkle or robot motif. Their center seam creates structure and becomes the stem of the J. The original Jenesis J-tail then continues beyond the brain, turning thought into useful work.

The result is simple enough to remain clear at favicon size, yet distinctive enough to own at larger brand moments. It feels intelligent, focused, approachable, and premium without becoming visually busy.

## Visual system

### Primary colors

- **Jenesis Blue:** `#7188FF` — clarity, imagination, and forward motion
- **Jenesis Ink:** `#171716` — confidence, focus, and premium restraint
- **Warm Light:** `#F7F7F4` — a softer, more human alternative to pure white
- **Warm White:** `#F3F1EB` — the preferred wordmark color on dark surfaces

### Wordmark

The JenesisAI wordmark uses a confident editorial serif with compact spacing. Its warmth balances the precision of the symbol and gives the identity a distinctive, established character.

Preferred typeface order:

`Iowan Old Style`, `Palatino Linotype`, `Palatino`, `Georgia`, serif

## Production usage

- Use the full symbol and wordmark for primary brand moments.
- Use the symbol alone for app icons, favicons, avatars, compact navigation, and loading states.
- Keep clear space around the symbol equal to at least one quarter of the mark’s width.
- Use the mark at **32 px** in the product sidebar. Avoid using the detailed symbol below **20 px** in interface layouts; use the optimized favicon asset for smaller browser contexts.
- Keep the blue tile and dark glyph together in standard product use.
- Do not rotate, stretch, outline, recolor individual parts, add gradients, or place effects behind the symbol.
- Do not separate the brain, center seam, or J-tail. Their continuity is the core of the idea.

## Motion principle

The animation follows the meaning of the mark:

1. The blue foundation arrives.
2. The brain is drawn as intelligence takes shape.
3. The center seam gives the thought structure.
4. The J-tail continues downward, turning intelligence into action.
5. The JenesisAI wordmark settles into place.

The motion should play once, remain calm, and complete quickly. It should never spin continuously or compete with the user’s work. Reduced-motion preferences must always be respected.
